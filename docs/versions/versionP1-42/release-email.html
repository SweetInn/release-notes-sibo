Release Notes - July 2026 (Version 42)

Dear Sweett Team,

This release is all about turning risk into opportunity. Our new Overbooking Management feature runs a cancellation risk calculation on specific bookings and, when the risk is high enough, safely opens those dates back up so you don't miss out on another guest.

We've also given cleaners and managers a proper home for those left-behind items with Lost & Found, so nothing gets lost in a side chat again.

On top of that, sales phone numbers now show up right on the web headers, making it easier for guests to reach your team and driving higher conversion by turning a passive visit into a direct conversation.

And over in the Back Office you can view, set, and regenerate Nuki lock admin codes in just a couple of clicks. And as always, we've been busy behind the scenes tightening up stability and squashing bugs across messaging, mobile reports, and website booking.

Overbooking Management (Cancellation Risk Based Availability)
Meet Overbooking Management. SIBO now runs a cancellation risk calculation on specific bookings, and when the risk is high enough, it quietly opens those dates back up so another guest can grab them, instead of waiting for a cancellation that might never actually happen. If a stronger signal confirms the stay, like a guest message, the dates close right away. And if the risk score simply drops over time, the chances of the original booking sticking around go up, which may call for relocating whichever booking was added in the meantime. If a real conflict does pop up, there's a dedicated dashboard with everything needed to sort it out fast. Built in collaboration with our BI team, whose cancellation risk model powers this feature.
Direct Sales Phone Numbers on Web Headers
Your sales number is now front and center on the web header, and it adapts to the guest's locale so each region sees the right number to call. One tap, one call, one less guest lost to a search engine. It is a small change on the page with a big impact on conversion.
Lost & Found
Say hello to Lost & Found. Cleaners snap a photo and jot a quick note the moment they find something, right from their phone. From there it is tracked start to finish: stored, returned, or disposed, with support able to see the full picture in the Unified Inbox whenever a guest asks. No more items disappearing into side chats.

SIBO

Occupancy & Revenue

  • Overbooking Management (Cancellation Risk Based Availability)
    Turn likely cancellations into extra bookings, without losing sight of the original guest.
    SIBO runs a cancellation risk calculation on specific bookings, and when a booking's risk is high enough, it can open its dates so another guest can book the same stay. The original booking is never cancelled, it stays live and is simply hidden from the calendar so we don't double count it. If a stronger signal confirms the stay, like a guest message, the dates close immediately to avoid a conflict. And if the risk score simply drops over time, the chances of the original booking sticking around go up, which may call for relocating whichever booking was added in the meantime. When a conflict does happen, there's a dashboard with everything needed to relocate, upgrade, or refund the affected guest, plus a notification so nothing is missed.
    Built in collaboration with our BI team, who developed the cancellation risk model behind this feature.

Field Operations

  • Lost & Found
    One clear process for handling items guests leave behind.
    Cleaners can report a found item from their phone with a photo and note. The item is linked to the property and booking automatically when possible. Managers get a single list of found items with filters, storage location, and guest contact, and can track each item through to return or disposal. Support can also see found items for a booking directly in the Unified Inbox.
  • Create Report for Non-Cleaner Staff
    Let every field team member report an issue, not just cleaners.
    Non-cleaner field staff, such as maintenance technicians, can now create a report directly from the SIBO mobile app using the same simple flow cleaners already have: a description and photo, no cleaning task required.

Direct Sales & Services

  • Phone Numbers on Web Headers
    Make it easier for guests to reach our sales team directly.
    Direct sales phone numbers are now displayed on the web header, adapted per locale so guests in each region see the right number, giving guests a quick way to call our team.

Security

  • Nuki Lock Admin Code Management
    Give Ops the same level of control over Nuki locks as we already have for Salto locks.
    From the property's Digital Keys panel, Ops can now view, set, or regenerate the admin code for a Nuki lock in a single action. The admin code is marked "Do not share" and is only ever visible internally, never to guests. Repeated change requests on the same lock are held while a previous change is still applying, so Ops always know the current state.

Stability

  • More Resilient Lock Syncing
    Keep lock syncing running smoothly even when one building has a problem.
    If access to one building is lost, it's automatically removed so it doesn't get stuck or block syncing. If a single building fails to sync, the rest continue as normal, and we get a clear reason for whichever building didn't sync.

  • Bug Fix Correct Message Now Sent to GuestsSIBO
    Fixed a rare case where sending a message in a conversation could deliver an old, unrelated draft instead of the message the agent actually typed.
  • Bug Fix Property Now Displayed on Reports From CleanersSIBO
    Reports created from a cleaning task in the SIBO mobile app now correctly show the related property and booking on the report details page.
  • Bug Fix Guest Count Now Capped to Room LimitWebsite/App
