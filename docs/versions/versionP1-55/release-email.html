Release Notes - October 2026 (Version 55)

Dear Sweett Team,

This release marks a major step forward in AI-powered operations, cleaning quality control, and operational automation.

Our biggest innovation is the introduction of the AI Cleaning Supervisor. For the first time, every cleaning can be automatically inspected by AI before completion, reducing the need for physical supervision and helping ensure consistently high cleaning standards across our properties.

We are also introducing significant improvements across daily operations:

  • Smarter breakfast operations: A unified daily board brings orders, payments and serving status together.
  • Automated lock maintenance: Guest availability coordination and automatic task creation simplify the entire maintenance workflow.
  • More independent partners: Partners can now create, update and send quotes, including authorized discounts.
  • Simplified guest services: Extra amenities are now managed directly from the booking.
  • Better guest experience: Breakfast ordering is easier and more flexible.

Together, these improvements move SIBO closer to our goal: automating routine operations, reducing manual work, and allowing our teams to focus on exceptions and guest experience.
AI Cleaning Supervisor
For the first time, a cleaning can be inspected from the photos before it is marked done. The cleaner sees what still needs attention, and the task finishes when every space passes inspection. A city chooses when to switch this on. The gain is less time checking apartments in person, and a more consistent standard from one property to the next. Cleaning Performance reads that same standard in guest reviews, as one score per cleaner, so managers can see who is excelling and who needs support.
Daily breakfast board
Orders, payments, and what has been served now sit on one board for the morning. Staff see who still needs to pay, mark breakfasts served, and a guest can scan a QR code to add more.

Website & App

Guest experience

  • Breakfast ordering
    Guests order the breakfast that fits this stay, without guessing which page to open.
    Included breakfast and a one-day breakfast for today or tomorrow now share one page, based on what is available for the booking at the time of order. A one-day order can cover up to the guest count plus 2. When only one breakfast can be ordered, the page shows that breakfast alone. The choice follows the stay and the breakfast cutoff. On checkout morning, guests can still order while a one-day breakfast is available.

SIBO

Cleaning quality

  • AI Cleaning Supervisor
    Inspect the clean from the photos, and see how each cleaner is doing from the guest reviews that follow.
    Until a city switches this on, a cleaning is still completed by tapping Start and Finish. With it on, the cleaner photographs the work, gets feedback on what still needs attention, and the task is done when every space passes inspection. They can keep working and are told when the result is ready. If a space still needs work, they correct it and check again. The standard is the same from one property to the next, and supervisors spend their time on the exceptions. Cleaning Performance carries that standard into one score from 1 to 5 for each cleaner, built from Airbnb, Booking.com, and NPS reviews, so the inspection and the rating tell the same story. The company score is the average of its cleaners. Managers see top performers, who needs attention, who improved most, and who is declining. Past reviews are already included. In-stay reviews are not included yet.

Breakfast operations

  • Daily breakfast board
    Run the morning from one board: who has paid, who is waiting, and what has been served.
    Staff used to see paid breakfasts only. Each order now holds the day, paid and still to pay, so the team can serve what is paid and collect what is not. The older breakfast list in SIBO still shows paid breakfasts only. Search by room, show the QR code, and the guest scans it to add more breakfasts.

Lock maintenance

  • Guest timing and automatic tasks
    Schedule the visit when the guest can be there, and let a ready report create its own task.
    During a stay, a technician visit used to be scheduled before anyone asked the guest. Battery and connection problems now follow one path. The guest says when they can be there, and the visit is set from that answer. When the report is ready to schedule, the task is created on its own. A report still waiting for the guest waits until they reply. Teams work these from the Reports queue.

Partner access

  • Partner quotes
    Partners can move a quote forward themselves, including an authorized discount.
    Creating a quote, and changing its price, used to stay inside Sweett. A partner can now create a quote, update it, and send it. This covers the Partner API and the private SIBO connection. A price change, as a percent, a fixed amount, a coupon, or a new total, needs permission to apply a discount. Listings, reservations, and business reports are not part of this access.

Guest services

  • Extra services on the booking
    Offer breakfast, early check-in, and late check-out without leaving the booking.
    Staff used to open these from older service links and separate task types. Extra Amenities now brings them together, with the price and the pay link, so the offer happens where the stay is managed.

    Already Paid now changes only when the money was actually returned. A PayPal refund stays pending until it succeeds or fails, and the payment date is the time of the refund.
  • Bug Fix Group payment timerWebsite/App
    Guests on a group checkout can no longer pay after the hold has ended. The page uses the same 9-minute timer as a single booking.
  • Bug Fix Create an accountWebsite/App
    On the Sweett app, a new guest can finish creating an account from Login. Submitting the form creates the account and moves them on.
  • Bug Fix Extra Amenities buttonSIBO
    Staff can open extra services on a confirmed booking even when the guest email is still not set.
  • Bug Fix Create Booking datesSIBO
    The stay on Create Booking keeps the dates chosen on the multi-calendar, so the nights booked match the nights selected.
  • Bug Fix Cleaner task countsSIBO
    Managers see the real workload for every cleaner, and the company total includes them all.
  • Bug Fix Cleaning time and costSIBO
    Cleaning Time and Cost in Cleaning Manager stay saved, including on a bulk save.
  • Bug Fix Cleanings windowSIBO
