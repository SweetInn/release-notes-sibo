Release Notes - August 2026 (Version 43)

Website & App

Multi-Room Booking

Guests can now book multiple rooms or apartments in a single transaction directly on our website — one search, one checkout, one payment.

The problem it solves
Until now, booking more than one room meant completing separate bookings one at a time. For guests who needed multiple rooms, this created a real and frustrating risk: by the time the first booking was confirmed, the second room might already be gone. Most guests simply didn’t try — and went to OTAs instead.

For corporate coordinators booking for a team, the problem was even worse. Booking six rooms meant entering payment details six times, managing six separate confirmations, and spending significantly more time than the task should ever require.

What changes for guests

  • Book multiple rooms in one go. Select all the rooms you need in a single session, no risk of losing availability between bookings.
  • One checkout, one payment. Enter payment details once regardless of how many rooms you’re booking.
  • One confirmation. A single booking reference covering all rooms, making it easy to manage and share.
  • Peace of mind. All rooms are held together and confirmed simultaneously, so there’s no race against availability.

Who benefits most

  • Families needing two or more rooms who previously wouldn’t risk booking on our site
  • Corporate coordinators booking for teams of any size — what used to take 30+ minutes now takes one
  • Group travelers — friends, wedding parties, event attendees — who need multiple units in the same property or area

Why this matters for direct bookings
Every booking made directly on our website avoids OTA commission entirely. Multi-room bookings are exactly the type of high-value reservations that were previously lost to OTAs — not because guests preferred them, but because our website couldn’t handle the need. That changes now.

The result
A faster, simpler, and more confident booking experience for anyone needing more than one room. More direct bookings, less commission paid to third parties, and a website that finally serves the full range of how our guests actually travel and work.

SIBO

Operations Dashboard

We’re introducing the Operations Dashboard — a real-time command center for city ops teams, available on desktop, mobile, and wall-mounted screens.

The problem it solves
Until now, ops teams had to manually navigate across multiple screens every day to find issues, prioritize them, and figure out what to do. This meant critical problems, a guest locked out, a cleaning running late, an angry in-stay guest, were often discovered too late, handled in the wrong order, or missed entirely.

What changes for ops teams
Every critical issue across all 500+ units is now surfaced automatically, in one place, ordered by urgency. No navigation. No hunting. No guessing what to do next.

  • Dramatically less time spent on triage. Issues are prioritized automatically so ops staff always know what to handle first.
  • Faster response to guests. Problems are visible the moment they occur, not after someone happens to find them.
  • Suggested actions on every alert. No need to figure out the next step; the dashboard tells you exactly what to do and gives you the tools to do it in one tap.
  • Automatic guest and cleaner communication. WhatsApp messages to cleaning company managers fire automatically on cleaning issues; guest messages are pre-templated and ready to send.
  • Nothing falls through the cracks. Unresolved critical issues escalate automatically and notify the city manager, so no alert goes unnoticed.
  • Full visibility on in-stay guests. Mood, VIP status, unanswered messages, and survey scores are visible at a glance, enabling proactive guest recovery before a bad review is written.

Three views, one source of truth

  • Desktop. Full detail per issue type, drill down to individual units, claim and resolve in one place.
  • Mobile. Prioritized list with push notifications, deep-linking directly to the relevant alert.

Critical alerts covered

  • Guest no-show watch
  • Cleaner running late
  • Cleaning projected to finish after check-in time
  • Locks with critically low battery
  • Lock codes not generated
  • Incomplete OCIs on arrival day
  • Guests that should have checked out but are still inside
  • Low in-stay review scores
  • Stuck reports and tasks
  • Unanswered messages sent by guests
  • Bookings cancelled in-stay

The result
Ops teams spend less time looking for problems and more time solving them. Guests get faster responses. Issues that used to sit unnoticed for hours are now resolved in minutes.

Overbooking Management

  • Double Booking multi-account filter
    Let ops review overbooking risk across several accounts at once, while keeping Risk Hold Settings scoped to one account at a time.
    The Double Booking account field now supports multi-select with search and removable chips. Apply filters the view to all selected accounts and stays disabled until at least one account is chosen. Settings opens Risk Hold Settings for one account: with a single selection the account is shown as a static label; with multiple selections an account switcher lists only those selected accounts and refreshes the modal when switched.

Unified Inbox

  • Per-account AI bot mode (Classification-only)
    Let each account run booking verification and routing without sending automated guest replies until its SOPs are ready.
    Each account can be set to one of four behaviours: No bot, Basic (legacy simple bot), AI Classification only, or Full AI. The behaviour is based on the account that owns the booking, not the shared inbox the message arrives through. In Classification-only mode, the assistant verifies the booking, selects the relevant SOP, and escalates to a human agent with no guest-facing reply. The agent sees an internal note with the SOP name and code plus the booking verification result. Account behaviour is configurable in Back Office account settings and takes effect for new messages without a deploy. Existing accounts keep their current behaviour until changed.
  • Compose email attachments
    Let agents attach files when starting a new email conversation, the same way they can on an email reply.
    When composing a new Email in Unified Inbox, agents can add one or more file attachments (multiple files, 16 MB total, any file type). Upload progress is shown, files can be removed before send, and Send stays unavailable while a file is still uploading. The guest receives the email with those files, and the new conversation shows the attachments in the inbox timeline. WhatsApp compose is unchanged and does not offer attachments.

Enhancements & Bug Fixes

  • Enhancement Non-Refundable urgency sortSIBO
    In Overbooking Management, the list now defaults to shortest time remaining before a booking becomes non-refundable, so the most urgent cases appear first. Staff can also click the Non-Refundable column header to sort ascending or descending.
  • Enhancement Overbooking relocation capacitySIBO
    In Overbooking Management, the relocation tool only proposes apartments that can accommodate the original booking’s full guest composition (adults, children, and infants). Units below guest capacity are excluded so ops cannot relocate into an undersized apartment.
  • Enhancement Overbooking low-risk confirmSIBO
    In Overbooking Management, when cancellation risk drops below the account threshold, the stay is confirmed and the booking moves to History. Ops no longer see low-risk bookings stuck on the Double Booking Current tab after the calendar hold is released.
  • Enhancement Overbooking rows per pageSIBO
    On Overbooking Management (Double Booking), the selected Rows per page value is preserved after table actions such as Confirm stay. The number of rows rendered and pagination now stay aligned with the chosen page size.
  • Enhancement Roster public holidays leaveSIBO
    In Roster / My Shifts, public holidays inside a leave request do not consume leave balance. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, employees receive a substitute day added to their leave allowance, without double-granting on calendar re-sync. Balances in Time Off reflect the updated entitlement.
  • Enhancement Roster leave from start dateSIBO
    In Roster / My Shifts, leave entitlement can accrue or prorate from the employee start date (for example BCN at 2 days per month). Mid-year hires receive the correct entitled days automatically in Time Off, without a manual override. Full-year employees still receive the full annual entitlement, and employee leave overrides remain available for exceptions.
  • Enhancement Roster pending leave request badgeSIBO
    Shows a small badge on the Roster menu item with the number of pending, approval-required leave requests from each manager’s direct reportees.
  • Bug Fix City page date availabilityWebsite/App
    On the website city page, search results for a selected date range showed almost the entire city inventory as available, even when those dates were not free. Results now align with Back Office availability for the same city and dates.
  • Bug Fix Quote path discount stackingWebsite/App
    On the quote conversion booking path, when membership was ON and a guest applied a higher coupon, pricing incorrectly stacked the membership discount and then applied the coupon on the reduced price. Only the highest discount applies across quote discount, membership, promotion, and coupon. Pricing now picks the best discount with no stacking.
  • Bug Fix Quote path promo field disabledWebsite/App
    On the website create-reservation flow entered via a quote, the promo code input was disabled while the Sweett Plus toggle was OFF. The promo code field now stays enabled regardless of membership toggle, so guests can enter a coupon and pricing can correctly choose the highest discount.
  • Bug Fix Leave balance with untracked typeSIBO
    In My Shifts → Time Off, when a leave type was untracked/unlimited and pending requests existed, switching the type to tracked (or cancelling those requests after) could corrupt pendingDays and show an incorrect remaining balance (e.g. 37 of 23 days). Cancelling requests that never incremented pending while untracked no longer drives pendingDays negative, and remaining days never exceed entitlement for tracked types.
  • Bug Fix PendingPayment task checkmarkSIBO
    On the booking details Tasks & Requests list, staff could mark a pendingPayment task as Done via the row checkmark, even though edit mode locks that status. The checkmark now follows the same rule as edit mode. pendingPayment tasks can only move to Done through the proper payment or activation flow, preventing unpaid OCI services from appearing complete.
  • Bug Fix AI Router courtesy reply orphan threadSIBO
    When the AI Router auto-sent a courtesy close reply, the message was delivered to the guest but saved into a new separate conversation instead of the one the agent sees. The auto-sent reply now files into the same conversation thread, so agents can see what the bot told the guest.
  • Bug Fix Cleaner create phone overwriteSIBO
    Creating a cleaner in Cleaning Manager with a different email but the same phone as an existing ops/staff user silently overwrote that account (same user ID, name and role changed to cleaner, email unchanged). Create now blocks phone conflicts with non-cleaner users instead of reusing and patching an existing staff account.
  • Bug Fix Cleaning reschedule stale end dateSIBO
    In Cleaning Manager, after assigning a cleaning (which can set an explicit endDate), changing only the start date left the old endDate in place. The cleaning appeared to start on the new day but finish on the original day. Rescheduling start date now clears or recalculates endDate so the slot stays on a single day.
  • Bug Fix Tax Rules cities tree crashSIBO
    On Tax Rules, the Cities filter crashed with Duplicate value undefined when a city had multiple accounts. Parent tree nodes now use unique values, and the city/account picker renders correctly for multi-account cities.
  • Bug Fix WhatsApp replies without a phone numberSIBO
    Some guests message on WhatsApp with a username instead of a regular phone number. Agents and the bot could receive those messages but could not reply. Replies now send successfully, the inbox no longer shows a fake phone number for those guests, and when a booking is linked, the booking phone is preferred for display.
