Release Notes - September 2026 (Version 45)

Dear Sweett Team,

As part of our ongoing push to grow direct bookings, this release introduces our brand new Agent Area, turning travel agents into a direct booking channel with affiliate-branded links to grow direct sales and deepen partnerships with longtime agents, while making it easy to welcome new ones.

The Operations Dashboard also gets sharper eyes, with new categories for upcoming high-value bookings and unresolved yesterday check-ins, catching risk before it ever reaches a guest.

You'll also spot a new live connection status indicator on that same dashboard, so you always know its real-time health at a glance.

On Airbnb, guest-initiated alteration requests are now captured and actionable straight from SIBO.

And in Roster, managers now get an instant push notification the moment a leave request needs their approval.
New Direct Booking Channel for Travel Agents
Turn travel agents into a direct booking channel: share, mark up, and convert stays without ever leaving the property page. With white-label access, agents copy an affiliate-branded link or download a brochure PDF to quote clients offline, then complete the booking on Sweett. With bookable access, they set their own markup within their capped share and copy an affiliate-branded link at the exact price the client will pay, commission built in and invisible to the guest. It's a whole new way to grow direct sales with travel agent partners old and new.
Operations Dashboard New Risk Categories
The Operations Dashboard now proactively flags two new risk moments: upcoming high-value bookings arriving today or tomorrow, and yesterday's check-ins that still have incomplete online check-in and an unsettled payment. And with the new live status indicator, there's no need to navigate away and check for updates, just stay on the dashboard and trust what you see in real time.

Website & App

Travel Agents & Direct Sales

  • Agent Area
    Give travel agents a direct booking channel to share, mark up, and convert stays, all without leaving the property page.
    Logged-in travel agents now see TRAVEL ADVISORS ONLY under Reserve once guests and dates are set. With white-label access, they copy an affiliate-branded, no-price link or download a brochure PDF for offline quoting, then complete the booking on Sweett. With bookable access, they set a markup on the net rate within their capped share, see the net rate, commission, and client total for Lite and Flex, and copy an affiliate-branded bookable link at that client total, the guest never sees the split. Clients open those links on The Stay Selection, where they can preview the stay, choose a rate when both are offered, check out, and pay one final price. After payment, Sweett keeps the net and the travel agent receives their commission via Stripe Connect. Guests can always find their trip again with Find your reservation (email plus a one-time code), landing on My bookings to complete online check-in and receive journey emails under The Stay Selection branding.

    A dedicated SIBO page for staff to create and manage customer agents is coming in a follow-up release; agent setup is handled directly by the team for now.

SIBO

Travel Agents

  • Affiliate Stay Visibility
    Give agents an instant way to spot affiliate bookings, without digging through filters.
    Affiliate stays booked through the Agent Area now appear as The Stay Selection in the OTA filter, with their own dedicated affiliate journey profile alongside the standard guest journey.

Operations Dashboard

  • Upcoming High-Value Booking Alerts
    Never miss a VIP arrival again: give agents an unmissable heads-up the moment a high-value booking is about to check in.
    The dashboard now has its own category for successful high-value bookings checking in today or tomorrow, based on the city's local calendar. Each item shows the booking and enough guest and unit context to act without leaving the list, and a Done button clears the alert for that stay. Cancelled bookings are removed automatically.
  • Yesterday Check-In: Unpaid & Incomplete OCI Alerts
    Close the loop on overnight risk automatically, catching unfinished check-ins and unpaid balances before they snowball.
    A new category flags successful bookings that checked in yesterday and still have both incomplete online check-in and an unsettled payment. Items clear automatically as soon as either condition is resolved, or if the booking is cancelled or marked as a no-show.
  • Live Connection Status Indicator
    See the dashboard's real-time health at a glance and trust the data in front of you, with no misleading static clock to second-guess.
    A compact status indicator next to the dashboard subtitle now shows Live, Reconnecting, or Disconnected, each with its own colour and label. A last-update time appears only in the degraded states, shown in the property's local time, with the full timestamp available on hover. The dashboard keeps showing the last known data while reconnecting, and a manual retry is available once retries are exhausted.

Roster

  • Leave Request Approval Push Notifications
    Put approvals a tap away: managers are instantly alerted the second a leave request needs their decision.
    When a worker submits a leave request that requires approval, their direct manager now receives a phone push titled New leave request, showing the requester's name, leave type, and start and end dates. Tapping the push opens Roster. No push is sent for leave types that do not require approval or when the worker has no direct manager, and approving, rejecting, or cancelling a request does not trigger a push.

Integrations

  • Guest-Initiated Alteration Requests (Airbnb)
    Turn every guest-initiated Airbnb request into an easy call, reviewable and actionable in seconds from SIBO or the AI chatbot.
    When a guest submits an alteration request directly in Airbnb, rather than one initiated on their behalf, it is now captured with the requested dates, guest counts, and price impact, and shown as a pending guest request. Agents or the AI chatbot can accept it, confirming the change in Airbnb, or decline it with an optional reason. Already-resolved requests cannot be acted on again.

Enhancements & Bug Fixes

  • Enhancement Airbnb Special Offers & Pre-ApprovalsSIBO
    Added backend support for creating Airbnb Special Offers and Pre-Approvals and for handling Airbnb's related webhooks. This lays the groundwork for upcoming SIBO and AI chatbot tools to send tailored offers directly to guests.
  • Bug Fix Rows Per Page Resets After ActionsSIBO
    Fixed an issue where changing Rows per page (e.g. to 50) on Double Booking and other new SIBO tables reverted to 10 rows and an incorrect page count after an action such as confirming a stay. The selected page size is now preserved.
  • Bug Fix Chatbot Delivery Reports Missing ItemsSIBO
    Fixed an issue where delivery reports created by the AI chatbot for missing amenities used a generic title and description, forcing the local operations team to open the guest chat to see what was requested. Reports now include the specific item(s) and quantities the guest asked for.
  • Bug Fix Accessibility Widget Not OpeningWebsite/App
    Fixed a production hotfix issue on old white-label booking sites where the accessibility widget was visible but did not open when clicked, restoring accessibility tooling for guests.
